# Rooms and resources

You book a meeting room or the installation's equipment with an event, and Ponistra makes sure nobody books the same room for the same time.

## How to use it

### Booking with an event

1. In the event, under {t:calendar.resources}, tick the room or resource you need.
2. What is already booked at the event's time is disabled and marked {t:calendar.taken}.
3. Press {t:tree.save}. If you remove the tick, the booking is released.

### Bookings in the calendar

1. In the calendar's left column, under {t:calendar.resources}, tick the rooms whose bookings you want to see. Beside each stand its place and its number of seats ({t:calendar.seats}).
2. The bookings appear in the calendar with the room's name before the event's title.

## How it works

The rooms and resources of the whole installation are entered by an administrator. A booking is part of the event: if you move the event the booking moves, and if you delete it the room is released. A recurring event books the room for every occurrence, checked a year ahead.

A booking is only shown in the calendar and does not open. For a private event, who booked the room stands instead of its title.

## Limits

- A room cannot be booked for a time when it is already taken; saving then reports an error.
- The choice of rooms shown in the calendar holds only in this browser.
