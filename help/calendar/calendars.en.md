# Calendars

You keep events in several calendars, each with a name and colour of its own; beside your own ones there are the calendars of your connected mail accounts.

## How to use it

### A new calendar and changing one

1. In the left column beside {t:calendar.calendars} press {t:calendar.settings}.
2. Press {t:calendar.newCalendar}, or right-click or long-press an existing calendar and choose {t:calendar.editCalendar}.
3. Type the {t:calendar.field.name} and choose the {t:calendar.field.color}.
4. Press {t:tree.save}.

### Deleting and following

1. In the calendar's window press {t:calendar.deleteCalendar} and confirm with {t:calendar.delete}.
2. A calendar that belongs to a connected account is not deleted but no longer followed: press {t:calendar.unfollow} and confirm with the same button.
3. The calendars you do not follow are listed under {t:calendar.unfollowed}; the {t:calendar.follow} button brings them back.

## How it works

Your own calendars live in Ponistra, and a new one gets the next colour in turn. The calendar of a personal mail account takes each event from the account once, and from then on that event is yours in Ponistra. An organisation calendar, which comes from the company's server, is only read: it is marked with the lock {t:calendar.readOnly}, it cannot be renamed or deleted, and only its colour changes.

A deleted calendar disappears with all its events. When you stop following an account's calendar, Ponistra removes its copy of the events, and they stay at the provider; following it again brings the calendar back at once, and its events with the next taking.

If an account cannot be reached, the account and the reason show below the list.

## Limits

- Deleting a calendar is permanent.
- An organisation calendar is not deleted or unfollowed in Ponistra.
