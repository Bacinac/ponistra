# Snoozing a message

A message leaves the inbox and comes back to its top at the moment you choose.

## How to use it

### Snoozing

1. Open the message and press {t:mail.snooze} on the bar above it. For several at once, [select them](help:mail/selecting) and press the same on the selection bar.
2. The question {t:mail.snoozeAsk} opens.
3. Choose {t:when.tomorrow} or {t:when.monday}, or enter a {t:when.day} and {t:when.time} and confirm.

### Snoozed messages

1. In the left column of Mail open the folder {t:mail.role.snoozed}. It appears after the first snooze.
2. The row shows when the message comes back, and the same is written above the open message.
3. To change the time, open the message, press {t:mail.snooze} and choose a new time.
4. To bring the message back at once, press {t:mail.unsnooze}.

## How it works

The server keeps the time, so the message comes back even when all your devices are off. It checks every fifteen seconds.

When the time comes, the message goes to the inbox, to the top of the list and unread, whichever folder it was snoozed from, and you get a [notification](help:account/notifications) as for a new message. The message's date does not change.

A snooze exists only in Ponistra. The copy at your mail provider stays where it was, so in the provider's webmail or another program the message is still in the inbox.

## Limits

- Messages in Sent, Drafts, Trash and Junk cannot be snoozed.
- Moving a snoozed message to another folder ends the snooze.
- The time must be in the future.
- Sender rules do not move snoozed messages.
- Without the network a message cannot be snoozed ([Working offline](help:account/offline)).
