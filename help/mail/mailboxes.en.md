# Shared mailboxes

A shared mailbox holds the accounts, mail, calendars and contacts several colleagues share, such as a sales or support address, apart from each one's own mail.

## How to use it

### Making one

1. Open Settings, then the mail accounts.
2. Under {t:mail.mailboxes} press {t:mail.mailboxNew}.
3. Type the mailbox's name and press {t:mail.mailboxMake}.
4. Under the mailbox's name connect its account as you connect your own.

### Sharing

1. Under {t:mail.mailboxes} long press or right click the name of a mailbox you own.
2. Choose {t:mail.mailboxShare}.
3. Add a colleague, a group or every user and choose the role: {t:access.role.read}, {t:access.role.edit} or {t:access.role.own}.
4. Change a role in the menu beside the person, and take access away with {t:access.remove}.

### Working in the mailbox

1. On the mail page, below the folders, under {t:mail.mailboxes} press the mailbox's name. The number of unread messages stands beside it.
2. Folders, messages and writing work as in your own mail. A message written in the mailbox goes from its account.
3. To go back to your own mail press {t:mail.mailboxOwn}.

### The mailbox's calendar and contacts

1. On the calendar or contacts page, in the left column under {t:mail.mailboxes}, press the mailbox's name.
2. The calendars and contacts of its accounts work as your own. An event written in the mailbox's calendar invites its attendees from the mailbox's account.
3. To go back press {t:calendar.mailboxOwn} or {t:contacts.mailboxOwn}.

### The mailbox on the phone

1. On a phone with the Ponistra app, or with its contacts and calendar connected by an app password, the mailbox's calendars stand beside yours, with the mailbox's name in brackets, and its contacts in a group bearing the mailbox's name.
2. With the role {t:access.role.read} the phone only reads them. With {t:access.role.edit} or {t:access.role.own} a change made on the phone goes to the mailbox, and the mailbox's account invites the event's attendees.
3. On Android a contact made on the phone goes to the mailbox when you put it in the mailbox's group.

### Renaming and removing

1. In the mailbox's menu choose {t:tree.rename} or {t:mail.mailboxRemove}.
2. Confirm the removal in the dialog {t:mail.mailboxRemoveTitle} Ponistra may first ask you to confirm who you are again.

## How it works

A mailbox owns its mail: its accounts, folders, rules, drafts, calendars and contacts are in no one's own mail and stay in the mailbox when a colleague leaves the organisation. No one signs in as the mailbox; colleagues reach it only through sharing.

The role {t:access.role.read} allows reading the mail, calendars and contacts. {t:access.role.edit} also allows moving, marking, deleting and writing from the mailbox's accounts, and changing its events and contacts. {t:access.role.own} also allows sharing, connecting accounts, the signature, the away answer, renaming and removing. A change one colleague makes the others see at once.

Ponistra notes which colleague opened which message of the mailbox. Where the installation follows openings, a sender in the same organisation sees that the message was opened in the mailbox.

Removing a mailbox deletes its accounts and all its mail from Ponistra, but what retention rules or a legal hold keep. Nothing is deleted at the mail provider.

## Limits

- A mailbox keeps at least one owner, so the last owner's role cannot be taken away or lowered.
- Only an owner can remove a mailbox.
- When the user who was a mailbox's only owner is erased, the user who takes over their documents becomes its owner.
- An iPhone shows only your own address book of the contacts connected by an app password, so the mailbox's contacts reach the phone through the Ponistra app on Android and DAVx5.
