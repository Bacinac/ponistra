# Document language

The document's language decides how numbers and dates are written in it, whatever the interface's language.

## How to use it

1. Click the document's title in the path and in the menu open {t:document.locale}.
2. Choose the language: Croatian, English, British English, German, French, Italian or Spanish.

## How it works

By the document's language the tables show numbers and dates. When you change the language, all the document's tables are calculated again. The date in the {t:field.date} field of the header and footer is written by it too, and Word and PowerPoint files saved from the document carry it as the text's language.

New and uploaded documents have Croatian as their language.

## Limits

- Only someone who may edit the document changes its language.
