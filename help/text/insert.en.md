# Inserting

The {t:editor.insert} menu on the toolbar and the slash “/” in the text insert pictures, tables, charts, contents, breaks, notes and other parts of a document.

## How to use it

### From the menu

1. Stand at a place in the text.
2. On the toolbar open {t:editor.insert} and choose what you insert.
3. For a {t:command.table}, move over the grid with the mouse or the arrows to the number of columns and rows you want, and choose it.

### With the slash

1. At the start of a line or after a space type “/”.
2. Go on typing part of the name, for example “/tab” for a table.
3. Choose the item with the arrows and press {k:Enter} or {k:Tab}. {k:Escape} closes the list.

### Contents

1. Stand where the contents should go and choose {t:command.contents} from the {t:editor.insert} menu.
2. The contents list the document's headings with their numbers.

## How it works

The menu holds {t:command.image}, {t:command.pdf} and {t:command.chart} as well as {t:command.link}, {t:command.table}, {t:command.contents}, {t:command.horizontalRule}, breaks, {t:command.speakerNotes}, {t:command.tab}, {t:command.header} and {t:command.footer}, and {t:command.footnote} and {t:command.endnote}. Only commands that make sense where you are are offered: in a table, a header or a note the menu is shorter.

The contents follow the headings of all the document's tabs and change as you change them. While the document has no headings, the contents say so. When no item matches the slash, the list says so, and “/” stays a plain character.

## Limits

- Only someone who may edit the document can insert.
- The slash opens the list only at the start of a line or after a space, so in addresses and dates it stays a character.
