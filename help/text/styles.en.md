# Styles and theme

A paragraph style sets a paragraph's font, size, colour, alignment, spacing and numbers, and the theme the fonts of the whole document.

## How to use it

### Paragraph style

1. Stand in a paragraph.
2. On the toolbar, in the {t:editor.style} menu, choose {t:command.paragraph}, {t:command.title}, {t:command.subtitle}, {t:command.heading1}, {t:command.heading2}, {t:command.heading3}, {t:command.heading4} or a style of your own.
3. You can also get a heading by typing: “# ” at the start of a line gives {t:command.heading1}, “## ” {t:command.heading2} and so on, down to four levels.

### Theme and styles

1. In the document's menu choose {t:styles.open} The {t:styles.title} dialog opens.
2. Under {t:styles.theme} choose {t:theme.ponistra}, {t:theme.classic}, {t:theme.office}, {t:theme.noto} or {t:theme.plex}, or separately the {t:styles.headingsFont} and {t:styles.textFont}, and the theme becomes {t:styles.ownTheme}.
3. Tick {t:styles.numberHeadings} for numbers before all headings.
4. On the left choose a style, and on the right change its {t:styles.font}, {t:styles.size}, bold, italic, {t:command.color}, {t:editor.align}, {t:styles.before}, {t:styles.after} and {t:styles.line}. The {t:styles.preview} shows the look at once.
5. Press {t:styles.apply}.

### A style of your own

1. In the dialog press {t:styles.new}.
2. Type its {t:styles.name} and choose the {t:styles.kind} the style builds on.
3. A style you no longer need you remove with {t:styles.delete}.

### Styles from another document

1. In the dialog press {t:styles.from}
2. Type the document's name and choose it in the list.
3. Above the styles it reads {t:styles.taken} with the document's name. Press {t:styles.apply}.

## How it works

A style's font can be the theme's headings font or text font, so it changes with the theme, or one particular font. Under {t:command.color}, {t:styles.automatic} means the theme's colour. A style imported from a file can keep its original colour, offered as {t:styles.original}.

Under {t:styles.numbering} a style gets numbers, letters or Roman numerals, or {t:styles.numberingNone}. Beside the numbers you type the text {t:styles.prefix} and {t:styles.suffix}, and lower headings can also carry the numbers of the headings above ({t:styles.nested}).

A change applies to all paragraphs of that style in the whole document, in every tab, only once you press {t:styles.apply}.

## Limits

- The basic styles cannot be deleted or renamed, only your own.
- Every style of your own must have a name.
- Only someone who may edit the document changes its styles.
