# Suggested changes

A proposal is a change not yet accepted: it shows in the text as inserted, deleted or changed until someone accepts or rejects it.

## How to use it

### Suggesting instead of changing

1. On the toolbar turn on {t:command.suggest}.
2. Write, delete and format as usual. Every change becomes a proposal.
3. Pressing {t:command.suggest} again brings you back to direct changes.

### Accepting and rejecting

1. Stand in a proposal. Above the line the {t:commandGroup.review} box appears with the kind of change, its author and date.
2. Press the tick ({t:action.proposalAccept}) or the cross ({t:action.proposalReject}).
3. For all proposals at once, choose {t:action.documentAccept} or {t:action.documentReject} on the toolbar.

## How it works

The kind of change reads {t:proposal.insertion}, {t:proposal.deletion}, {t:proposal.replacement} or {t:proposal.formatting}. Proposals are also made by Claude's actions on the document, for example comparing with a version, and by outside agents you have let in: what they write into the document arrives as a proposal and does not change the text until you accept it. A [comparison with a file](help:text/compare) makes proposals too, under the file's name as their author.

An accepted proposal becomes plain text, and a rejected one disappears without a trace in the text. A right click on a proposal also offers accepting or rejecting all proposals in its menu.

## Limits

- Only those who may edit the document suggest, accept and reject. With the right to read, the proposal's box shows only the kind, author and date.
- The buttons for all proposals stand on the toolbar only while the document has proposals.
