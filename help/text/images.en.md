# Pictures and PDF in a document

You insert pictures and PDF files from your folders or your computer into a document, change their size, alignment and description, and you can also edit a picture.

## How to use it

### A picture

1. In the {t:editor.insert} menu choose {t:command.image}.
2. In the {t:image.insert} dialog press {t:file.fromComputer} or choose a picture from your folders.
3. You can also paste a picture or drag it into the text.
4. Press the picture and drag the handle at its edge for its size. You choose its alignment in the {t:editor.align} menu.

### Commands on a picture

1. Press the picture. The {t:commandGroup.image} menu appears on the toolbar.
2. {t:command.imageAlt} opens a field for a short description of what the picture shows. Press {t:tree.save}.
3. {t:command.imageEdit} opens a dialog for cropping, turning, tone and marks. Press {t:image.apply}.
4. {t:command.deleteImage} removes the picture from the document.

### PDF

1. In the {t:editor.insert} menu choose {t:command.pdf}
2. In the {t:pdf.insert} dialog press {t:file.fromComputer} or choose a PDF from your folders.
3. Press the PDF, and in the {t:commandGroup.pdf} menu on the toolbar choose {t:command.pdfPages} In the {t:pdf.pages} dialog, type {t:pdf.firstPage} and {t:pdf.lastPage} and press {t:tree.save}.
4. {t:command.pdfOpen} opens the PDF on a page of its own, and {t:command.deletePdf} removes it from the document.

## How it works

An inserted picture or PDF is a copy of the file: later changes to the original file do not change the document. A file chosen from your computer is kept only in the document, not in your folders. Pictures pasted from the web are fetched by the server. Pictures Word keeps in files on your computer cannot be pasted, so Ponistra tells you to insert them as files.

An edited picture is saved as a new picture in the document, and the one before stays in the versions. Screen readers and search read the picture's description.

## Limits

- Some kinds of picture cannot be edited, and the dialog says so.
- A PDF in a document shows only the range of pages you choose, from the first to the last.
