# Links

A link in the text leads to a web page, an email address, a phone number, another Ponistra document or an exact place in a document.

## How to use it

### A new link

1. Choose the text that becomes the link, or just stand at a place in the text.
2. Press {t:command.link} ({k:Mod-k}) in the {t:editor.insert} menu.
3. Type the {t:link.text} and the {t:link.href}, or under {t:link.find} type a document's name and choose it.
4. Press {t:tree.save}.

### An existing link

1. Stand in the link. Under it the address appears with buttons.
2. Press the address or {t:link.open} to open it, {t:link.edit} to change it, or {t:link.remove} to leave the text without a link.

### A link to a place in a document

1. Stand in a paragraph or choose some text.
2. With a right click choose {t:command.blockLink}.
3. Paste the link into a message, another document or a conversation.

## How it works

A link to a Ponistra document or page opens in the same browser tab, and any other in a new one. A link to a place opens the document at that tab and that paragraph, and if text was chosen, leads to that very text.

When no text is typed, the link shows the address, and when a document was chosen from the search, its name.

## Limits

- The address must start with `http://`, `https://`, `mailto:` or `tel:`, or lead to a Ponistra document. Otherwise it is not saved, and the dialog says so.
- A link to a place opens only for someone who has access to the document.
