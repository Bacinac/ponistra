# Formatting text

You give the chosen text bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, subscript or superscript, a colour and a highlight.

## How to use it

### The letters' form

1. Choose the text.
2. On the toolbar press {t:command.bold} ({k:Mod-b}), {t:command.italic} ({k:Mod-i}), {t:command.underline} ({k:Mod-u}) or {t:command.strike} ({k:Shift-Mod-x}).
3. {t:command.subscript} ({k:Mod-,}) and {t:command.superscript} ({k:Mod-.}) lower or raise the text.
4. The same button or shortcut takes the form away again.

### Colours

1. Choose the text.
2. Open the {t:commandGroup.color} or {t:commandGroup.highlight} menu on the toolbar.
3. Choose a colour: {t:color.gray}, {t:color.red}, {t:color.orange}, {t:color.yellow}, {t:color.green}, {t:color.teal}, {t:color.blue} or {t:color.purple} for text, and for highlighting also {t:color.pink}.
4. {t:color.auto} gives the text its style's colour back, and {t:color.none} removes the highlight.

### Removing formatting

1. Choose the text.
2. Press {t:command.clearFormatting} ({k:Mod-Space} or {k:Mod-\}).

## How it works

When nothing is chosen, the form holds for the text you are just starting to write. A toolbar button is highlighted when the chosen text already has that form.

{t:command.clearFormatting} removes the letters' form and colours, but not links, and gives paragraphs back their style's alignment, indents and tab stops. Font, size and spacing come from the [paragraph style](help:text/styles), so they are changed there, for all paragraphs of the same style at once.

When you look for commands in the search box, the colours are the commands {t:command.color} and {t:command.highlight} with the colour's name.

Colours show adjusted to the interface's light or dark theme, and in exports and printing they take their original value.

## Limits

- Font and letter size are not chosen for single words but for the paragraph style.
- Colours are chosen only from those offered.
