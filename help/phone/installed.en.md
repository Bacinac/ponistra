# The installed app

You install Ponistra from the browser on a computer or a phone, and it opens in a window of its own, with an icon that counts what is unread.

## How to use it

1. Install Ponistra from the browser's menu or from the notification settings, as [notifications](help:account/notifications) describes.
2. Open it by its icon on the computer or phone.
3. A long press or right click on the icon offers a new note and speaking a command or note.

## How it works

The app's icon shows the number of unread messages in your mail accounts' inboxes and of unread messages in conversations (in channels only mentions and replies in threads you follow); while the app is closed, the number changes by the notifications that arrive. The window's title is the name of the open page, without Ponistra's name, which the browser itself writes at its start.

The app keeps to one main window: launching it again brings the open window to the front, and a link opened from outside opens in it. A meeting, a presentation or a file in a window of its own hands such an opening to the main window; where none is open, they show a message and the button {t:launch.open}.

The installed app can open files from the computer through "Open with": documents Ponistra imports and PDF. Each file opens in a window of its own, alone across it, with a thin bar along the top: the Ponistra logo opens the menu of modules ({t:rail.label}), followed by the name, actions, help, the account menu ({t:frame.account}) and {t:device.close}; the tabs and headings stand in a column below the bar. The main window stays where it was; a module, the settings or help chosen in that window open in the main window, and the file stays in its own. While it loads, the window shows {t:launch.receiving} If the main window that received it is closed meanwhile, the window says {t:launch.lost} A browser that does not let a window open without your click keeps the file in the main window, in the {t:launch.held} box at the bottom right, where the {t:launch.window} button opens it; allow pop-ups for Ponistra in the browser and they open by themselves.

The file becomes a document with no folder: it is not among My documents, and you find it among the recent documents and in search, where {t:tree.unplaced} stands in place of its folder. Its content is saved as you change it, as in every document. {t:tree.saveInto} in the header opens {t:tree.saveTitle}, where you choose the folder and confirm with {t:tree.saveHere}; only then does the document get a place. A document with no folder that nobody opens or changes for 30 days goes to the trash, from where you can restore it.

While you are not signed in, a PDF opens alone across the window, without the rest of the app, with the page, find and the file's headings in a column beside it, and is not sent to the server; several files opened at once sit in the {t:device.files} bar at the top, and {t:device.close} leads to signing in. Other files wait until you sign in. An email link (`mailto:`) that reaches Ponistra opens as a new message from your default account, with the recipients, subject and text from the link.

You share content from other apps into Ponistra as [sharing with Ponistra](help:phone/share) describes.

## Limits

- The number on the icon and opening files depend on whether the browser and the system support them.
- The icon's menu takes your account's language only once the browser refreshes the installed app, which can take a few days.
- On Android, Ponistra also has [an app of its own](help:phone/android), which in addition keeps contacts and calendars in the phone.
