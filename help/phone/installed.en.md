# The installed app

You install Ponistra from the browser on a computer or a phone, and it opens in a window of its own, with an icon that counts what is unread.

## How to use it

1. Install Ponistra from the browser's menu or from the notification settings, as [notifications](help:account/notifications) describes.
2. Open it by its icon on the computer or phone.
3. A long press or right click on the icon offers a new note and speaking a command or note.

## How it works

The app's icon shows the number of unread messages in your mail accounts' inboxes and of unread messages in conversations (in channels only mentions and replies in threads you follow); while the app is closed, the number changes by the notifications that arrive. The window's title is the name of the open page, without Ponistra's name, which the browser itself writes at its start.

The app keeps to one main window: launching it again brings the open window to the front, and a link or file opened from outside opens in it. A meeting or a presentation in a window of its own hands such an opening to the main window; where none is open, they show a message and the button {t:launch.open}.

The installed app can open files from the computer through "Open with": documents Ponistra imports and PDF. The file is saved among your documents as an upload is, and a single file is also opened. An email link (`mailto:`) that reaches Ponistra opens as a new message from your default account, with the recipients, subject and text from the link.

You share content from other apps into Ponistra as [sharing with Ponistra](help:phone/share) describes.

## Limits

- The number on the icon and opening files depend on whether the browser and the system support them.
- The icon's menu takes your account's language only once the browser refreshes the installed app, which can take a few days.
- On Android, Ponistra also has [an app of its own](help:phone/android), which in addition keeps contacts and calendars in the phone.
