# Sharing with Ponistra

From another app on the device, share files, text or a link into Ponistra and save them to a folder, a note or an email.

## How to use it

1. In the other app choose sharing and Ponistra among those offered.
2. The {t:share.title} page opens with the shared text and the list of {t:share.files}, with each file's size.
3. Choose what to do with it:
   - {t:share.toFolder} saves the files in the folder you choose, and the shared page as a link in it; confirm the folder with {t:share.saveHere}.
   - {t:share.toNote} makes a note with the title, the text and the link.
   - {t:share.toMail} opens a new message with the title as its subject, the text, and the files attached.
   - {t:share.discard} throws away what was shared.

## How it works

{t:share.toConversation} opens the list of your conversations; in the one you choose the text and files wait in the message field until you send it.

Ponistra is offered for sharing when it is installed on the device, as the [Android app](help:phone/android) or as the [installed app](help:phone/installed). As the shared page it takes the link the app sent, or where there is none, the last web address in the text. The message is written from your default mail account, as [writing a message](help:mail/composing) describes.

Once what was shared is saved, sent or thrown away, the page forgets it.

## Limits

- {t:share.toFolder} is offered only when files or a page were shared, {t:share.toNote} only when text or a link was shared, and {t:share.toMail} only when you have a mail account.
- When nothing was shared, it says: {t:share.nothing}
