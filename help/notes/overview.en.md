# Notes

A note is a short text you jot down on the spot, from any page, and its title is its first line.

## How to use it

### A new note

1. On the [notes](/notes) page press {t:notes.new}, or press {k:Alt-n} on any page.
2. The note opens as a text document. Write; it saves by itself.

### The list of notes

- Each note in the {t:notes.title} list shows its title, the start of its text and the date of its last change.
- The star beside a note adds it to your favourites; favourites stand at the top of the list.
- The menu on a note, or a right click on it, offers favourites and {t:tree.toTrash}.
- For several notes at once, long-press one and add the others with a tap or click; the choice bar offers {t:tree.move} to another folder and {t:tree.toTrash}.

## How it works

A new note is called {t:notes.untitled} until you type its first line, and then the title follows the text's first line. When you rename a note yourself, the title no longer follows the text. Notes are ordered from the last changed, and favourites stand before the rest.

Notes live in your notes folder among your documents, so the search finds them and they are shared like other documents. A note is also made by voice, as [voice commands](help:start/voice) describes, and from content shared from a phone, as [sharing with Ponistra](help:phone/share) describes.

## Limits

- While you have no note, it says: {t:notes.empty}
- A note moved to another folder is no longer on this list.
