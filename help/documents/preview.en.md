# Item preview

The preview beside a folder's list shows the content and details of the chosen item without opening it.

## How to use it

1. In a folder, press the {t:preview.title} button above the list.
2. Press an item in the list. The preview shows its beginning and details.
3. When several items are chosen, the preview shows their number and total size.
4. The same button, or closing the panel, hides the preview.

## How it works

For a document its beginning is shown: headings, paragraphs, tables and pictures. An empty document says: {t:preview.empty} A picture and a PDF show small, a PDF by its first page, a text file by its beginning, and video and sound can be played in the preview itself.

Under the content stand {t:explorer.kind}, {t:explorer.size}, {t:explorer.updated}, {t:explorer.updatedBy} and {t:access.title}. An imported document also gives {t:preview.source} with the {t:import.original} button.

Whether the preview is on is remembered in that browser. When no item is chosen, it reads: {t:preview.none}

## Limits

- In a {t:confined.label} folder the original file cannot be downloaded from the preview.
- On a narrow screen the preview is not offered.
