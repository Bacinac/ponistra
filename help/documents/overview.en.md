# Documents overview

Documents holds your texts, tables, presentations and files in folders, together with those you share with others.

## How to use it

![A folder of documents with a preview of the chosen item](shot:documents-folder)

### Moving between folders

1. Open {t:rail.documents} on the bar of modules.
2. In the left column choose {t:tree.root}, {t:tree.company} or {t:tree.shared}. The arrow beside a folder opens its subfolders. On a phone the {t:frame.folders} button opens the folders, and {t:favourites.title}, {t:notes.title}, {t:links.title}, {t:boards.title}, {t:vault.title} and {t:trash.title} stand at the top of the page.
3. Click a folder in the list, or go to it and press {k:Enter}. A double click opens it even when the preview beside the list is on.
4. The path above the list leads back to each folder above.

### A new item

1. Press {t:create.new} above the list, or right-click an empty place in the list.
2. Choose {t:tree.newDocument} or {t:create.folder}.
3. Type the {t:tree.name} and press {t:tree.create}. A new document opens at once.
4. With {t:create.upload} or {t:create.uploadFolder} you upload files from your computer, and with {t:create.scan} paper photographed with a phone ([Scanning](help:pdf/scan)).

### Order of the list

1. Press a column's heading: {t:tree.name}, {t:explorer.kind}, {t:explorer.updated}, {t:explorer.updatedBy} or {t:explorer.size}.
2. Pressing the same column again reverses the direction.

## How it works

An item's kind shows by its icon and in the {t:explorer.kind} column: {t:kind.folder}, {t:kind.document}, {t:kind.table}, {t:kind.slides}, {t:kind.file}, {t:kind.image}, {t:kind.text}, {t:kind.video}, {t:kind.audio}, {t:kind.board} or {t:kind.other}. For other files the column also names the file's extension. A document is a table when its first tab holds tables alone, and a presentation when it has slide layouts of its own.

The {t:access.title} column shows who besides you can open the item: {t:access.onlyMe}, {t:access.everyone} or the badges of people and groups. The {t:access.wider} mark means the item has wider rights than the folder it is in. A padlock beside the name marks a folder {t:confined.label}.

A document or folder you make in {t:tree.root} belongs to you. What you make in someone else's folder takes that folder's rights. The chosen order is remembered in that browser and holds for every folder. An empty folder shows: {t:tree.empty}

## Limits

- {t:create.new} is not available in a folder where you only have the right to read.
- When an item does not exist or you have no access to it, the page shows a message and the link {t:tree.backToRoot}.
- On a narrow screen some columns are not shown.
