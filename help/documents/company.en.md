# Company folders

Company folders are shared folders the administrator lays out, and each user sees those they have been given access to.

## How to use it

1. In the left column of the {t:rail.documents} module press {t:tree.company}.
2. Open a folder and work in it as in any other, according to the role you have.

## How it works

The administrator makes the company folders and decides who has which role in them. What you make inside a company folder takes on its rights, so everyone with access to the folder sees it at once.

The {t:tree.company} item in the left column appears only once you have access to at least one company folder.

## Limits

- You cannot rename, move or trash a company folder itself, and its access is not changed from the {t:access.title} panel.
- When you have access to no company folder, the page says so.
