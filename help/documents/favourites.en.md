# Favourites

Mark the documents, files, folders, links and boards you open often with a star, and find them in one place.

## How to use it

1. In an open document or file press the star in the header, {t:favourites.add}.
2. In a list long-press a row or right-click it and choose {t:favourites.add}.
3. For several items at once choose them and pick {t:favourites.add} in the menu of the choice. When all are favourites already, {t:favourites.remove} stands there.
4. Open {t:favourites.title} in the left column. Items are grouped by the place they are in.
5. You remove an item with {t:favourites.remove}.

## How it works

Favourites are yours: a colleague you share a document with does not see your star, nor does it change their list. An item that goes to the trash or is no longer shared with you leaves the list, and returns if it does. On the Today page, a box of favourites stands below the other boxes.

While the list holds nothing, the page says: {t:favourites.empty}

## Limits

- Mail messages have their own Favourites in Mail, and contacts their own star in Contacts. This page does not show them.
- The calendar, tasks and chats have no star.
