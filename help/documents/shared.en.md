# Shared

The {t:tree.shared} page gathers the documents and folders others have shared with you, and on a second tab those you have shared with others.

## How to use it

1. In the left column of the {t:rail.documents} module press {t:tree.shared}.
2. At the top choose {t:tree.sharedWithMe} or {t:tree.sharedByMe}.
3. You open the items and work with them as in any folder.

## How it works

Under {t:tree.sharedWithMe} stands every item that someone else's access brought you to, given to you, to a group you are in or to everyone. When you have access to a folder, only that folder is shown, not everything in it. Company folders do not show here, as they have a place of their own.

Under {t:tree.sharedByMe} stand your items that someone besides you can open, again only the topmost such item.

When the list is empty, the page says so.

## Limits

- What you may do with an item depends on your role: with the right to read you cannot change, move or delete.
- Items in the trash do not show here.
