# Table design

A table's design sets the lines, colours and emphasis of the table's parts in one choice, instead of formatting cell by cell.

## How to use it

### Design

1. Stand in the table and in the {t:commandGroup.table} menu open {t:command.tableDesign}.
2. Choose {t:tableDesign.grid}, {t:tableDesign.lines}, {t:tableDesign.bands}, {t:tableDesign.header} or {t:tableDesign.plain}.

### Table parts

1. In the same menu, below the designs, turn {t:tablePart.header}, {t:tablePart.totals}, {t:tablePart.firstColumn}, {t:tablePart.lastColumn}, {t:tablePart.rowBands} or {t:tablePart.columnBands} on or off.
2. The parts turned on are emphasised as the chosen design draws them.

## How it works

A new table gets the {t:tableDesign.grid} design. When you change the design, the parts turned on stay on. The design of a table imported from Word, Excel or PowerPoint is kept. When saved to Excel, the design becomes an Excel table where Excel can draw it, and otherwise goes over cell by cell.

When you look for commands in the search box, the designs are under {t:command.tableDesign} and the parts under {t:command.tablePart}.

## Limits

- With {t:tableDesign.plain} the table has no design, so its parts cannot be turned on.
