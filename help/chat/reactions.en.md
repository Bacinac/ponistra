# Reactions

You answer a message in a conversation with an emoji, without writing a new message.

## How to use it

1. Hover over a message and press {t:reactions.react}, or long-press or right-click the message.
2. Choose one of the six usual emoji, or {t:reactions.more} for all the others.
3. In the {t:reactions.choose} window look for an emoji in the {t:reactions.search} field or choose it by group, for example {t:reactions.group.smileys-emotion}, {t:reactions.group.people-body} or {t:reactions.group.symbols}.
4. Below the message, in the {t:reactions.list} list, a click on someone else's reaction gives you the same one, and a click on yours takes it back.

## How it works

Below the message each emoji stands once, with the number of people who gave it; hover over it to see who. Everyone in the conversation sees reactions at once, and a meeting's guests can react too. Emoji are found by their names in the interface's language, and those with a skin colour offer every tone.

A mail message is reacted to the same way, with the button in the open message's header. Such a reaction goes as a reply to everyone in the message and is kept in your sent mail; a program that does not show reactions shows it as a short message.

## Limits

- A reaction to a mail message cannot be taken back.
- If nothing matches the search, it says: {t:reactions.none}
