# Files in a conversation

A message can carry files from your computer or from Documents, and a link to an item of Ponistra is shown as that item's card.

## How to use it

### Sending

1. Beside the message field press {t:chat.attach} and choose {t:chat.fromComputer} or {t:chat.fromDocuments} You can also drag files anywhere onto the conversation or paste them into the message field.
2. Above the field the list {t:chat.attached} shows how far each upload has gone. {t:chat.removeFile} takes a file out of the message.
3. Press {t:chat.send}. A message may have no text at all.

### Viewing and saving

1. Press the thumbnail or the card in a message. The file opens over the conversation, which stays behind it.
2. {t:file.download} saves the file to your computer, and {t:share.toFolder} saves it among your documents, in the folder you choose with {t:share.saveHere}. After that {t:chat.savedAt} leads to the saved file.
3. {t:tree.open} opens a file from Documents.

## How it works

A file from your computer belongs to the conversation and everyone in it sees it, until you save it to a folder yourself. A file from Documents stays one original: the members of the conversation get the right to read it, which shows in the file's access, and a new member gets it on joining. The right is taken back when a member leaves or the message is taken back; a right you gave someone by hand stays.

Pictures and videos are shown as thumbnails, PDFs and documents as a card with their beginning, name and size. In the list of conversations a message without text is shown as the number of its files.

Links in a message are live. A link to an item of Ponistra is shown as the same card, but only to those allowed to read that item. Someone without a right to an attached file sees its name and {t:chat.fileClosed}.

## Limits

- In Documents you can choose only files you own.
- Files from a vault folder cannot be given to a conversation.
- One message can carry at most 20 files.
- A guest in a meeting sees files from a computer but not those from Documents, and cannot send files.
