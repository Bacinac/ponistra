# Channels

A channel is a lasting, named place for a topic or a team. Replies in it go into threads, and you are told only of what concerns you.

## How to use it

### New channel

1. Open [the conversations](/chat) and press {t:channel.channels} in the left column.
2. Press {t:channel.new} and enter the {t:channel.name}.
3. If you wish, turn on {t:channel.private} and enter colleagues under {t:channel.people}.
4. Press {t:channel.create}. The channel opens.

### Finding an open channel and joining it

1. Press {t:channel.channels} in the left column and, if you need to, type part of the name into {t:channel.filter}.
2. Open the channel. You can read its messages without being a member.
3. To write, press {t:channel.join}.

You enter a private channel when one of its members adds you through {t:invite.add}.

### Mentioning someone

1. In the field for writing, type `@` and the start of a name.
2. Choose the person from the list with the arrows and {k:Enter}, or with the mouse. In a channel the list also has {t:channel.everyone}, which means all the channel's members.

The list offers only the conversation's members. A name you mention stands out in the message.

### Threads

1. Beside a message press {t:thread.reply}, or press and hold it or right-click it and choose the same action.
2. The thread opens beside the channel, and in its place on a phone. Write your reply in its field.
3. Under a message with a thread stands the number of replies; pressing it opens the thread.

### Managing a channel

The channel's menu has {t:invite.add}, {t:tree.rename} (in the window {t:channel.rename}), {t:chat.leave} and, for whoever created the channel and for an administrator, {t:channel.remove}.

## How it works

Every colleague of the installation sees an open channel, and its name is unique in the installation. A private channel exists only for its members and does not appear in the list of channels.

In a channel you get a phone notification and a number on the rail only when someone mentions you, mentions all the members, or when a reply arrives in a thread you follow. An ordinary message only makes the channel's row stand out. In direct conversations, groups and meeting conversations every message is announced, as before, and one message brings at most one notification.

You follow a thread if you wrote the message being answered, replied in the thread or are mentioned in it. Replies stay in the thread and do not appear among the channel's messages. When you edit a message, only those you newly mention are told.

An open channel stays even when everyone has left it, with its whole history, until whoever created it or an administrator deletes it. A channel with messages within a retention period or on legal hold cannot be deleted. A private channel is gone when its last member leaves.

## Limits

- Threads exist only in channels and have one level: a reply does not get a thread of its own.
- Only a member of a channel can open a meeting from it.
- Taking a message back also removes the replies in its thread.
