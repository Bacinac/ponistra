# Messages in a conversation

In a conversation you write short messages that everyone in it sees at once, and you can edit or take back your own.

## How to use it

### Writing

1. In the {t:chat.write} field type the message.
2. On a computer press {k:Enter} or {t:chat.send}; {k:Shift-Enter} starts a new line. On a phone the keyboard's Enter starts a new line, and you send with the {t:chat.send} button.
3. For older messages, press {t:chat.earlier} at the top of the {t:chat.messages} list.

### Editing and taking back

1. Beside your message open {t:chat.actions}, or long-press or right-click it, and choose {t:chat.edit}. Above the field it says {t:chat.editing}.
2. Change the text and press {t:tree.save}, or {t:tree.cancel} or {k:Escape} to give up.
3. {t:chat.takeBack} asks for confirmation and removes the message.

## How it works

A message reaches everyone in the conversation at once, and the other members get a notification on their phone. An edited message carries the mark {t:chat.edited} for everyone, and a message taken back disappears for everyone. Consecutive messages of the same person within five minutes stand together, and others' messages show the writer's name; a meeting's guest is marked {t:meeting.guest}, and a message of a user who is no longer there shows {t:comment.formerUser}.

In a conversation you can mention a member: type `@` and the start of a name, then choose them from the list, and their name stands out in the message. In a [channel](help:chat/channels) not every member is told, only those mentioned and those following the thread.

The conversation is marked read while the page is in front of you. Under your newest message stand the names of those who have read it; a channel does not show this. Messages are plain text, without formatting, and [files](help:chat/files) go along with them.

## Limits

- You can edit and take back only your own messages.
- A message taken back cannot be restored.
- A message within a retention period an administrator set cannot be taken back, and one on legal hold cannot be edited either; the reason is written in the menu under {t:chat.takeBack}.
