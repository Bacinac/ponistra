# Administration

Administration gathers the pages that apply to the whole installation, which only an administrator opens.

## How to use it

1. Press your initials in the top right corner, {t:frame.account}, and choose {t:admin.title}. The first page, {t:users.title}, opens.
2. Choose a page in the left column: {t:users.title}, {t:resources.title}, {t:workdays.title}, {t:relay.title}, {t:providers.title}, {t:identity.title}, {t:ai.title}, {t:retention.title}, {t:journal.title} or {t:health.title}.
3. On a phone the list of pages is a page of its own. An open page goes back to the list with {t:frame.close}.

## How it works

What is set here holds for every user of the installation. The settings of your own account stay in {t:settings.title}. Each page of the administration can also be opened from the search box by typing its name.

## Limits

- {t:admin.title} is in the menu only for an administrator; for anyone else its address opens {t:settings.title}.
