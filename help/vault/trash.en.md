# The vault's trash

Deleted items of all collections you can edit wait in the vault's trash, where you restore them or delete them for good.

## How to use it

### Deleting an item

1. In an open item choose {t:tree.toTrash} in the menu of actions.
2. For several items choose them in the list and press {t:tree.toTrash} above the list.

### Restoring and deleting for good

1. In the vault's left column open {t:trash.title}.
2. Beside an item press {t:trash.restore} for it to go back to its collection.
3. Or press {t:trash.purge} and confirm in the dialog {t:trash.purgeTitle}
4. For several items long-press one and add the others, and the actions show on the choice bar. A right click on an item offers the same.

## How it works

The vault's trash is apart from the trash of documents. Each item says which collection it came from and when it was deleted, and the list starts with the newest. An item in the trash shows neither in its collection nor in the search, and the members of a shared collection see it in their vault's trash if they can edit in the collection.

Deleting for good removes the item together with its attachments and cannot be undone.

## Limits

- Anyone who can edit in the collection moves items to the trash and restores them, and only the collection's owner deletes them for good. In My vault you are the owner.
- The trash does not empty by itself.
- An empty trash shows: {t:trash.empty}
