# Boards

A board is a page of HTML you open in Ponistra, such as a plan, a tracker or an overview, most often written for you by an outside agent.

## How to use it

### The list of boards

1. In the Documents side column open [boards](/boards).
2. Boards stand by groups: first your groups by name, then your boards without a group under {t:links.ungrouped}, then the boards others share with you, by the place they come from.
3. The board you press shows beside the list, and the first one while you choose none. A double press opens the board; on a phone one tap is enough.

### Grouping

1. A right click or long press on a board opens a menu with {t:boards.group}
2. Type the group's name and press {t:tree.move}. A group of that name is made if there is none yet.

### An open board

- The board's menu offers {t:file.open}, which opens the board alone in a new tab, and {t:board.source} for its HTML; {t:board.shown} returns to the view.
- {t:access.share} opens the board's access, as for any document.
- {t:version.list} shows the board's earlier pages, as [board versions](help:boards/versions) describes.
- {t:board.agent} opens a chat with an agent in the code workspace, as [sending a board to an agent](help:boards/agent) describes.

## How it works

A board is written by an agent you connected to Ponistra, as the article [What an agent can do](help:agents/tools) describes. The Boards page holds the boards in your folder of boards, whose folders are its groups, and those in folders of boards others share with you.

A board shows as soon as an agent writes a new page. A board may keep data of its own, such as a list's ticks: anyone who may read the board reads them, and whoever may edit it changes them. When that data changes, an open board reads it again.

A board is closed in a frame of its own: it sees nothing else of Ponistra, leads nowhere and loads only libraries from a few public servers.

## Limits

- A board's page is at most 1 MB.
- While you have no board, it says: {t:boards.empty}
