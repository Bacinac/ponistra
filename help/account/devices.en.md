# Signed-in devices

The list of browsers you are signed in on, where you can sign out one you do not recognise or no longer use.

## How to use it

1. In Settings open {t:devices.mine}.
2. Look through the list: {t:devices.device}, {t:devices.signedIn} and {t:devices.seen}.
3. Beside a browser you do not recognise press {t:devices.end}.

## How it works

Each browser shows the browser and system and the network address it signed in from. If the browser cannot be recognised, it shows as {t:devices.unknown}. The list is in order of last activity, which is noted at most every 5 minutes.

The browser you are looking at the list in is marked {t:devices.this} and cannot be signed out from here; for it use signing out in the {t:frame.account} menu. A browser signed out asks for a sign-in the next time it opens Ponistra.

A browser that keeps documents for working offline carries the mark {t:offline.marked}; it is turned on on the device itself, in the section {t:offline.title} above the list.

While there is no sign-in, it says: {t:devices.none}

The installation's administrator sees in its journal every sign-in, sign-out and ending of sessions, with the address it came from.

## Limits

The list shows only sign-ins in a browser. Apps that sign in with an app password are listed under app passwords and removed there.
