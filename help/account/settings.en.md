# Settings

Settings gather in one place everything that applies to your account: signing in, devices, mail, artificial intelligence and documents.

## How to use it

1. Press your initials in the top right corner, {t:frame.account}, and choose {t:settings.title}. Typing {t:settings.title} into the search box does the same.
2. Choose a page in the left column. Under {t:settings.account} are {t:settings.signIn} ({t:signIn.choice}, {t:passkeys.title} and {t:password.title} on one page), {t:devices.mine}, {t:appPasswords.title} and {t:notifications.title}.
3. Under {t:settings.mail} are {t:mail.accounts}, {t:mail.senders}, [{t:away.title}](help:mail/away) and [{t:collections.title}](help:mail/collections).
4. Under {t:settings.ai} are {t:agents.title}, {t:consents.title} and {t:translation.title}.
5. Under {t:settings.documents} is [{t:drive.title}](help:account/google-drive).
6. On a phone the list of pages is a page of its own. An open page goes back to the list with {t:frame.close}.

## How it works

The settings on these pages apply to your account on every device, except those that explicitly concern the browser you are in, such as that browser's notifications.

The pages that apply to the whole installation are not in Settings: an administrator opens them from {t:admin.title} in the same {t:frame.account} menu. The time sheets of the code workspace are set on the {t:rail.projects} page ([Writing the time worked](help:agents/hours)).

The address of a page that does not exist opens the first page of the settings.

## Limits

Only an administrator opens the installation's pages. The interface language and theme are chosen in the {t:frame.account} menu, not in Settings.
