# Editing a contact

You add a new contact or change an existing one in one form: the name, the company, addresses and numbers, the birthday and a note.

## How to use it

### Name and work

1. Press {t:contacts.new} or, in an open contact, {t:contacts.edit}. The form's heading is {t:contacts.new} or {t:contacts.editTitle}.
2. Type the {t:contacts.field.given} and the {t:contacts.field.family}.
3. {t:contacts.more} opens {t:contacts.field.prefix}, {t:contacts.field.middle}, {t:contacts.field.suffix}, {t:contacts.field.name} and {t:contacts.field.nickname}.
4. For work type the {t:contacts.field.organisation}, the {t:contacts.field.department} and the {t:contacts.field.title}.

### Addresses and numbers

1. Under {t:contacts.reach} each row has a kind and, in the {t:contacts.label} menu, a label such as {t:contacts.label.work}, {t:contacts.label.home} or {t:contacts.label.mobile}; {t:contacts.label.none} leaves the row without one.
2. The buttons below the list add a new row: {t:contacts.kind.email}, {t:contacts.kind.phone}, {t:contacts.kind.address} or {t:contacts.kind.url}.
3. An address is typed in parts: {t:contacts.address.street}, {t:contacts.address.code}, {t:contacts.address.city}, {t:contacts.address.region} and {t:contacts.address.country}.
4. {t:contacts.removePoint} beside a row removes it.

### Birthday and note

1. Under {t:contacts.field.birthday} type the {t:contacts.day}, choose the {t:contacts.month} and, if you know it, type the {t:contacts.year}.
2. In the {t:contacts.field.note} type what you want to remember about the person.
3. Press {t:tree.save}.

## How it works

A new contact starts with one row for email and one for a phone. If you do not type the full name, it is put together from the title, the given name, the middle name, the family name and the suffix. A phone also has the labels {t:contacts.label.main}, {t:contacts.label.fax}, {t:contacts.label.pager} and {t:contacts.label.other}.

A birthday without a year is kept as a day and month only, and birthdays show in the calendar. A change reaches a phone that reads the address book with its next sync. You add a contact's photo in the open contact's menu, as [the contacts overview](help:contacts/overview) describes.

For a colleague from the organisation's directory, their details from the directory stand beside yours; the company and job title from the directory are not changed here and carry the mark {t:contacts.heldByDirectory}.

## Limits

- A note has at most 20,000 characters, and the full name 500.
- What the organisation's directory holds is not edited here.
