# The organisation's directory

You add colleagues from the organisation's directory to your contacts, and their details from the directory refresh by themselves.

## How to use it

### Adding a colleague

1. In contacts, in the page's menu, choose {t:contacts.colleague} The same opens with the {t:contacts.searchDirectory} button below the list when you type at least two letters into the contacts search.
2. In the {t:contacts.colleagueSearch} field type the start of a given or family name.
3. Beside the person found press {t:contacts.add}. The colleague becomes a contact and opens at once. Beside a colleague who is already in your contacts stands {t:contacts.open}.

### A colleague in your contacts

1. In the contacts list a colleague from the directory carries a building sign described as {t:contacts.fromDirectory}.
2. In the open contact, under {t:contacts.directory}, stand their addresses and numbers from the directory, with the name of the server they come from.

## How it works

The organisation's directory exists when the organisation's mail account is connected over ActiveSync. The directory is searched only by the start of a given or family name, so Ponistra never walks all of it; correspondents the directory confirms as colleagues become contacts by themselves.

An added colleague stays linked to the directory and their details from the directory refresh every night, so a new number or job title arrives by itself. Your own addresses and numbers for the colleague stand beside those from the directory, and the directory's details are kept by the organisation's IT, not by you.

## Limits

- The search needs at least two letters; with fewer it says: {t:contacts.colleagueShort}
- If nobody matches, it says: {t:contacts.colleagueNone}
- If the directory's server does not answer, {t:contacts.unanswered} shows beside its address
