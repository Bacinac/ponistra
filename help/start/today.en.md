# Today

The Today page shows in one place the day's schedule, the tasks due, the mail that waits and the documents worked on lately.

## How to use it

### The day at a glance

1. Press {t:rail.today} on the bar of modules, or the Ponistra mark at its top.
2. Below the date it says how many events you have today, which holiday it is and how many birthdays there are.
3. In {t:today.schedule} press an event to open it. Pressing a birthday opens the contact of the one celebrating.
4. With {t:rail.calendar} in the schedule open today in the calendar.
5. In the small month in the left column press a day to open it in the calendar. Change months with the {t:calendar.previousMonth} and {t:calendar.nextMonth} arrows.

### A new event and task

1. For an event today press {t:calendar.newEvent} in the header.
2. For a task due today press the plus beside the heading {t:tasks.due.title}.
3. With {t:tasks.title} open all tasks.

### Mail and documents

1. In {t:today.mail} press a message to open it, or {t:rail.mail} for all mail.
2. In {t:today.recent} press a document, or {t:rail.documents} for all documents.
3. If you have added anything to {t:favourites.title}, that box is below too. The number beside its heading opens the whole list.

## How it works

The schedule shows all-day events first, then the others by start time. A red line marked {t:today.now} with the current time stands between past and coming events, and past ones are faded. The next or running event is highlighted, with how long until it starts; if it has an online meeting, a {t:meeting.join} button is beside it. A cancelled event is struck through. An invitation you have not answered yet is marked {t:calendar.awaiting}. Calendars, holidays and birthdays you hid in the calendar do not show here.

Tasks due are the unfinished tasks whose due date is today or has passed.

The mail that waits is the unread messages in the inboxes from people you have written to before and the messages you starred in the last 2 weeks, newest first. Snoozed messages are not among them until they come back. 5 messages show, and the number beside the heading says how many there are in all.

Recent documents are the 10 documents and files you may read, ordered by their last change, whoever made it. At most 8 items of the favourites show.

The button for a new event shows only if you have a calendar that can be written to.

## Limits

- While there are no events, it says: {t:today.noEvents}
- While no message waits, it says: {t:today.noMail}
- While there are no documents, it says: {t:today.none}
- When no task is due, it says: {t:tasks.due.none}
