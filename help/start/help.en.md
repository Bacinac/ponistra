# Help

The help describes every function of Ponistra: how to use it, what happens meanwhile and what its limits are.

## How to use it

### Help about the page you are on

1. Press {k:F1} or the question mark in the header ({t:help.title}), or ask the microphone a question about Ponistra.
2. The article about the page you are on opens. If there is none, the contents of the help open.

### Contents and searching

1. Open the [help](/help) by the question mark in the header or through the search box.
2. On a wide screen the contents are in the left column, and the page holds all chapters with a short description of each article. On a phone the contents are the page itself, and you close an article by the cross above it.
3. Search for articles in the search box too: the hits are in the {t:search.help} group.
4. Links in an article lead to other articles or straight to the page of the application the article is about.

## How it works

The help is part of the installation and comes with every new version, so it describes exactly the version you use. It shows in the interface's language. The names of buttons and menus in the articles are read from the interface itself, so they always match what you see, and shortcuts show for your device, for example {k:Mod-k} as Ctrl+K or ⌘K. Pictures follow your language and theme.

Articles for administrators are seen only by the installation's administrators.

## Limits

The help describes Ponistra, not the outside services it connects to, such as mail providers.
