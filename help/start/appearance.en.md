# Theme and language

The Ponistra interface can be light or dark and in Croatian or English, and the choice holds on every device of yours.

## How to use it

### The account menu

1. Press the circle with your initials in the right corner of the header.
2. Under {t:frame.theme} choose {t:frame.theme.system}, {t:frame.theme.light} or {t:frame.theme.dark}.
3. To give a meeting's page or the {t:rail.projects} page a theme other than the rest of Ponistra's, choose {t:frame.theme.light} or {t:frame.theme.dark} under {t:frame.theme.meetings} or {t:frame.theme.projects}. The default is {t:frame.theme.ponistra}. Only the owner of the code workspace sees {t:frame.theme.projects}.
4. Under {t:frame.language} choose the language.

### The search box

1. Type "theme" or "language" into the search box.
2. Choose a command, for example {t:frame.theme} with a theme's name. The current choice is disabled.

## How it works

{t:frame.theme.system} follows the light or dark mode of your operating system and changes along with it, without reloading. The light and dark themes change only the interface's colours; the colours in a document's content stay as they are.

The meetings theme and the Projects theme hold while that page is open, in Ponistra's tab and in its window of its own; the terminal on the Projects page follows the Projects theme. {t:frame.theme.ponistra} means the page has the same theme as the rest of Ponistra.

The themes and language are kept with your account, so another device takes them as soon as you sign in on it. The browser keeps them for the sign-in page too. The page from which a document is printed to PDF always has the look of paper, whatever the theme.

## Limits

The language changes only the interface and the help. The content of documents, messages and conversations stays in the language it was written in.
