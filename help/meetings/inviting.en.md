# Inviting to a meeting

You invite the installation's colleagues by ringing them, and others by a link that reaches them by email; someone can ring you into a meeting too.

## How to use it

### Inviting

1. In the meeting, or before joining a new one, press {t:invite.add}.
2. In the {t:invite.people} field type the people, as you would a message's recipients.
3. Press {t:invite.send}. Below the field {t:invite.rung} shows with the names of the colleagues being rung and {t:invite.written} with the addresses of the others.
4. You can also send the link yourself: {t:meeting.copyLink} keeps it, and {t:invite.share} sends it through an app of the device, if the device offers that.

### Who answered

1. In the meeting, under {t:invite.rings}, each person shows {t:invite.declined}, {t:invite.missed} or {t:invite.ringing}
2. {t:invite.again} beside a person rings them again.

### When you are rung

1. A call with the name of the person calling appears in every open Ponistra, and a ring sounds.
2. {t:meeting.join} opens the meeting, and {t:ring.decline} declines the call.

## How it works

Colleagues are rung in every Ponistra they have open and on their phone, until they answer, decline or 45 seconds pass. The colleagues you rang join the meeting's conversation, whether they answer or not. People outside the installation get from your account, by email, a link of their own that lets them straight into the meeting, without waiting at the door.

A meeting added to an event is not invited to here: those invited to the event already have the link, so the meeting's header offers only {t:meeting.copyLink}. A conversation's meeting rings everyone in the conversation, as [the conversations overview](help:chat/overview) describes.

## Limits

- Sending the link by email needs a mail account in Ponistra.
- Ringing lasts 45 seconds; whoever does not answer carries the mark {t:invite.missed}
